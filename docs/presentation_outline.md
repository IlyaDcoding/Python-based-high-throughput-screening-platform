# HEC-DB Screening Presentation Outline

1. **Project question and scope**  
   Introduce Topic 7, the team, and the question of which high-entropy ceramic specimens to shortlist for thermal barrier coating research.
2. **Why thermal barrier coatings**  
   Explain the roles of thermal conductivity and thermal expansion. Define the scope as screening bulk zirconates for follow-up.
3. **Dataset and traceability**  
   Show the specimen and observation counts, the four sources of numerical observations, and a single record with original units, conditions, DOI and table location.
4. **HEC-DB compatibility and data validation**  
   Show a long-format row and a wide-table import example. Explain unit conversion, source-specific measurements, duplicates and missing values.
5. **Screening scenario**  
   List family, application, material class and origin. Identify the selected measurement cohort for each property. Describe the inclusive limits and the exclusion of four specimens with different measurement conditions.
6. **Numerical method and verification**  
   Display min–max equations for both directions, the normalized weights and the matrix–vector product. Walk through the manual score calculation for GDHEYZO and mention the automated checks.
7. **Verified candidate ranking**  
   Use the generated ranking and property figures. Describe the three rankings and the scope of the tested protocol. Cite Huang et al., Table 2. [Primary publication](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-6412/16/1/79)
8. **Weight sensitivity**  
   Show the actual sensitivity figure. Describe the stable first rank and the two perturbations in which the other specimens exchange places.
9. **Limits and follow-up**  
   Explain the need for matched high-temperature measurements and coating-specific validation. End by showing the reproducible CLI command and the CSV/XLSX shortlist.
